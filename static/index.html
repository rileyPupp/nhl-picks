NHL Picks

Three skaters a night, the same three for everyone: one star, one middle-of-the-lineup player, one depth player. Call how many points each gets. 1 pt for calling point / no point right, +2 for the exact number (3+ counts as 3). A player who doesn't dress doesn't count.

Pick % = points you earned ÷ points you could have earned, on the nights you played. Once you make any pick on a night, every finished game (and every drawn skater who played) counts, so skipping hard games doesn't help. Nights you sit out don't count against you.
